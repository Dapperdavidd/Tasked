# Tracked product requirements

Tracked turns a plan source into a dated program with daily checklists, per-enrollment scores, streaks, and heatmaps. A capped standing list holds unrelated ongoing habits without polluting the bounded program.

Primary product constraints:

- The checkbox is the product.
- Ingestion is the moat.
- Private by default.
- Consistency over perfection.
- Finite commitments, except the capped standing list.
- Separate scores for separate commitments.

In scope for v1:

- Ingestion and program generation.
- Confirm screen.
- Today view with program and standing sections.
- Standing list cap and drift prompt.
- Scoring and streaks with forgiveness.
- Heatmap.
- Two composed daily notifications.
- Invite-link cohorts with presence only.
- Completion artifact.
- Lapse return path.

Explicit non-goals:

- Social primitives.
- Public profiles or discovery.
- Combined scores.
- Standing list cohorts.
- Raising the five-task standing cap.
- Routing standing tasks through ingestion.
